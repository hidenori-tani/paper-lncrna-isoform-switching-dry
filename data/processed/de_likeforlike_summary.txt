Interpretation:
  The rank test keeps a large blind spot even when fed the SAME TMM-normalised
  counts the NB models use, so the spread is driven by the statistical model
  (power), not by the TPM input scale.
  rank/TPM 15.3%  ->  rank/TMM-CPM 17.3%  ->  DESeq2 1.3%
